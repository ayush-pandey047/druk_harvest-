# 02 — Audience & User Research

## 1. User Groups
| Group | Main need | What they look for |
|---|---|---|
| Households | Fresh, trusted produce | Product list, seasonality, how to order, delivery area |
| Restaurants and hotels | Consistent supply, bulk quantity | Wholesale enquiry, origin, reliability, lead time |
| Retailers | Quality products to stock | Range, packaging, minimum quantity, contact |
| Health-conscious consumers | Know what they eat | Origin, honest farming info, recipes |
| Visitors and tourists | Authentic local food and gifts | Honey, grains, traditional goods, stories |
| Local farmers | A fair route to market | "Supply with us" info, partnership steps, contact |

## 2. Personas (illustrative)
- **Pema, 34, working mother in Thimphu (Household).** Wants weekly fresh vegetables without market trips. Uses a phone. Cares about price clarity and origin.
- **Chef Karma, 41, hotel kitchen (Business buyer).** Needs steady supply of seasonal vegetables and red rice. Wants quantity options and a quick quote request.
- **Sonam, 52, farmer in a valley village (Supplier).** Low digital confidence. Wants simple language, clear steps, phone/WhatsApp contact.
- **Emily, 29, visitor (Tourist).** Looks for honey and local goods to take home. Wants English content and a story to connect with.

## 3. What Users Want
- Understand what Druk Harvest is, instantly.
- Appetising product imagery with name, origin and season.
- Trust: see the people behind the food (farmer stories) and honest information.
- A simple ordering path with no confusion about what happens next.
- Fast loading and smooth scrolling on a phone.
- Easy contact: phone, email, WhatsApp-style link, location.
- Simple English, with optional Dzongkha names for identity.

## 4. User Journeys
- **Household:** Home → hero and farmer highlight → Products → filter (Vegetables / In Season) → product detail → "Add to enquiry" → Enquiry → submit → confirmation.
- **Business buyer:** Home → How It Works → Wholesale section → Enquiry (Business) → submit.
- **Farmer:** Home → "Supply with us" → How It Works (supplier steps) → Supplier enquiry.
- **Curious visitor:** Home → Our Story → Farmer Stories → Recipes → Products.

## 5. Pain Points and Design Answers
| Pain point | Design answer |
|---|---|
| "Is this really local?" | Origin tag on every product and farmer profile |
| "What is available now?" | Seasonal filter and "In season" badge |
| "How do I order?" | 3-step How It Works and an always-visible Enquiry button |
| "Is the site slow?" | Optimised images, light animations |
| "Can I trust them?" | Farmer stories, transparent sourcing, no exaggerated claims |

## 6. Suggested Research Activities
- Quick survey (5 questions) with 8–10 people on how they buy fresh produce.
- Short interviews with one household buyer and one restaurant/hotel contact.
- Review 3–4 farm-to-table websites for patterns and gaps.
- Record findings in a one-page summary to support design decisions.
