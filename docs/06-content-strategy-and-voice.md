# 06 — Content Strategy & Voice

## 1. Voice
Fresh, honest, community-minded, approachable, dependable. Short sentences, warm words, no jargon.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Grown by small farms in the valleys of Bhutan." | "100% organic and certified." |
| "We work directly with farmers." | "We have transformed thousands of lives." |
| "In season now." | "The best produce in the world." |

## 2. Sample Copy
- **Hero:** *From Bhutanese farms to your table.* Fresh seasonal produce and traditional goods, grown by small farmers and shared with care.
- **Mission:** To make local food easier to find, and to give farming communities a dependable way to reach customers.
- **Trust note:** We tell you where your food comes from, who grew it and when it is in season. We only make claims we can stand behind.
- **Enquiry success:** Thank you. We have received your enquiry and will contact you to confirm availability and delivery.
- **Empty basket:** Your enquiry list is empty. Browse our products and add what you like.

## 3. Content Inventory (minimum)
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Products | 12–15 (at least 3 per category) |
| Farmer profiles | 4–6 |
| Recipes / stories | 4–6 |
| FAQs | 8 |
| Illustrations or images | Hero, journey steps, farmer scenes, product images |

## 4. Image & Media Guidance
- Prefer custom SVG illustrations or properly licensed images; label as illustrative.
- Show hands, tools, terraces, fields, baskets, markets with dignity and realism.
- Alt text on every image describing what is shown.

## 5. Language
Primary English. Small Dzongkha touches (e.g., *Kuzuzangpo la*) with spelling checked by a Dzongkha speaker.

## 6. Content Workflow
1. Draft → 2. Claims check (file 14) → 3. Tone check → 4. Spelling check → 5. Mark placeholders → 6. Final.
