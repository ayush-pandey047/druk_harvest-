# 03 — Company-Side Requirements

## 1. Business Messages
1. We work directly with small Bhutanese farmers.
2. We celebrate local produce and traditional food products.
3. We are transparent about sourcing, ordering and delivery.
4. We support farming communities and fair, responsible business.
5. We serve both individual families and businesses.

## 2. What the Company Wants to Showcase
- **Farmers' work:** a visual journey from field to table (sowing, tending, harvest, sorting, packing, delivery).
- **Product range:** vegetables, fruits, grains, honey, locally made goods.
- **Process:** sourcing, quality checking, ordering, delivery.
- **Community impact:** told honestly through stories, not unverified numbers.
- **Seasonality:** what is fresh now and what is coming next.

## 3. Business Goals → Website Features
| Business goal | Website feature |
|---|---|
| More customer enquiries | Persistent CTA, enquiry basket, clear form |
| Attract suppliers | Supplier route and form |
| Build trust and brand | Farmer profiles, honest sourcing page |
| Educate customers | Recipes, seasonal updates, product origin |
| Reach hotels and restaurants | Wholesale section, business enquiry type |

## 4. Content the Company Must Avoid
- "Certified organic", "100% chemical-free", "carbon neutral", "fully sustainable" unless verified.
- Invented statistics (e.g., "500 farmers helped"). Use qualitative statements or clearly marked placeholders.
- Real people's photos or names without permission.

## 5. Company Brand Values
Freshness · Honesty · Community · Dependability · Local identity

## 6. Company-Side Content Needs
- About the founders/team (illustrative).
- Sourcing policy in simple words.
- Delivery arrangements (placeholder areas and timing).
- Contact details and social links (placeholders).
