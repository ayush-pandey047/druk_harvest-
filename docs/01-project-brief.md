# 01 — Project Brief & Business Requirements

## 1. Summary
Druk Harvest connects small Bhutanese farmers with households, restaurants, hotels, retailers and visitors. The website must communicate **freshness, trust, local identity and responsibility**, make products easy to explore, and explain the relationship between the business, farmers and customers.

## 2. Goals
1. Introduce the business and its farm-to-table approach within 10 seconds of landing.
2. Put **farmers at the centre** of the story.
3. Let visitors explore products by category, season and origin.
4. Guide visitors towards an **enquiry or order request** (primary conversion).
5. Attract potential farmer-suppliers via a clear "Supply with us" route.
6. Feel contemporary and smooth while celebrating Bhutanese food and farming.

## 3. Success Measures
| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| First meaningful paint | under 2 s on average mobile connection |
| Lighthouse Performance (mobile) | 90+ |
| Lighthouse Accessibility | 95+ |
| Animation frame rate | steady 60 fps, no visible jank |
| Enquiry form reachable | within 2 clicks from any page |
| Responsive | no horizontal scroll from 320px to 1920px |

## 4. Scope
**In scope:** brand identity, responsive multi-page frontend, animations, static product catalogue, farmer profiles, recipes/seasonal updates, enquiry form (frontend validation only).

**Out of scope (for now):** backend, database, payments, user accounts, real order processing, CMS, delivery tracking.

## 5. Constraints
- HTML/CSS/JavaScript (or a frontend framework). No server.
- Do **not** claim organic certification or any unverified sustainability claim.
- Content is fictional; mark sample farmer names, quotes and numbers as illustrative placeholders.
- Must stay fast on slow mobile networks.

## 6. Assumptions
- Content (images, text) will be created or licensed by the student.
- Hosting will be a free static host (Vercel or Netlify).
- Primary language is English, with small Dzongkha touches.
