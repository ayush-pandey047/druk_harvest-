# 14 — Claims Guardrails

The assignment forbids organic certification claims and unverified sustainability claims.

## Safe Wording
- grown locally · sourced from small farms · grown in [valley/district]
- in season · traditional · handmade locally
- "We work directly with farmers."
- "We tell you where your food comes from."

## Avoid (unless evidence is shown on the site)
| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| organic, certified organic | Implies certification |
| pesticide-free, chemical-free | Needs verification |
| eco-friendly, sustainable, carbon neutral | Unverified environmental claim |
| fair-trade | Implies certification |
| "best", "purest", "healthiest" | Unprovable superlative |
| Invented numbers (farmers helped, tonnes saved) | Misleading |
| Medical or health benefit claims | Needs evidence and regulation |

## Placeholder Marking
Mark illustrative content in the interface footer or near the content:
> Farmer names, quotes and figures on this site are illustrative for a design project.

## Review Step
Before every release, search the codebase and content for the avoid-list words and fix or remove them.
