# 07 — Brand Identity Guide

## 1. Brand Essence
*Rooted in Bhutan. Fresh to your table.* A contemporary food brand with a respectful nod to Bhutanese culture and landscape.

**Personality:** Fresh · Honest · Community-minded · Approachable · Dependable

## 2. Logo Concept
- Wordmark "Druk Harvest" in a friendly modern serif or rounded typeface.
- Symbol idea: a simplified **terraced hillside forming a leaf/sprout**, or a sun rising over a field. Avoid copying the national emblem or dragon imagery directly, out of respect.
- Versions: full colour, one colour, reversed (on dark), icon only (favicon).
- Clear space equal to the height of the letter "D"; minimum icon size 24px.
- Misuse examples to show: stretching, recolouring, low contrast, busy backgrounds.

## 3. Colour Palette
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Terrace Green | #3F7D3A |
| Primary dark | Forest Deep | #1F4A2B |
| Accent | Saffron Gold | #E0A526 |
| Accent 2 | Chilli Red | #C8402F |
| Background | Rice Cream | #FAF6EC |
| Surface | Soft Sand | #EFE7D4 |
| Text | Earth Charcoal | #2B2620 |

Use Rice Cream and green for most of the interface; saffron and red sparingly for highlights and buttons. Check every text/background pair for WCAG AA (4.5:1).

## 4. Typography
- **Headings:** warm serif (Fraunces or Playfair Display).
- **Body/UI:** clean sans-serif (Inter or Nunito Sans).
- Desktop scale: H1 56px, H2 40px, H3 28px, body 17–18px, small 14px.
- Load two families only, subset, `font-display: swap`.

## 5. Iconography & Patterns
Line icons with rounded ends. A subtle textile or cloud-inspired pattern at low opacity.

## 6. Photography / Illustration Style
Natural light, earthy tones, close-ups of produce and hands, wide landscapes, consistent colour grading. For illustration: flat shapes with soft grain.

## 7. Brand Applications to Design
Business card · product label · packaging tag · social post · delivery bag · invoice header.
